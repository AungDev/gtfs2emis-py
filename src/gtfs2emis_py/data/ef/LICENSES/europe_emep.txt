EMEP/EEA air pollutant emission inventory guidebook (2019)
============================================================

Source: European Environment Agency
https://www.eea.europa.eu/publications/emep-eea-guidebook-2019

The packaged `europe_emep.parquet` table is a transformation of emission-factor
data derived from the EMEP/EEA guidebook. It is redistributed under the EEA
legal notice / Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY 4.0)
terms: https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/legal-notice

Attribution: European Environment Agency (EEA), EMEP/EEA air pollutant emission
inventory guidebook 2019.

Changes: the guidebook workbook was converted to a Parquet table, column names
were preserved from the R reference where practical, and rows were merged across
the 2007/2013/2016/2019 editions used by the reference package.

The EEA notice excludes third-party content from its CC-BY permission. Users
must check the guidebook's own copyright page for any third-party material
before redistributing this table further.

The table was obtained through ipeaGIT/gtfs2emis v0.1.2 (MIT code), commit
0dbca7d779857145888ab695f36dc9e71ff57d98. The R package's MIT licence covers its
code, not the underlying EEA data; this notice governs the data.
